# DeepSeek Harness Brand Asset Usage Guidelines

English | [中文](BRAND_GUIDELINES.zh.md)

To maintain the long-term health of the DeepSeek Harness ecosystem, avoid user confusion, and make related resources easier to find and identify, follow these guidelines:

- In project descriptions, you may use “DeepSeek Harness” to truthfully and accurately describe your project's relationship with DeepSeek Harness, for example, “built on DeepSeek Harness” or “compatible with DeepSeek Harness.”
- If you want a project name to reflect its association with the DeepSeek Harness ecosystem, we recommend using the abbreviated “DSH” designation.
- Avoid using the full “DeepSeek Harness” trademark directly in a project name. “DeepSeek Harness” is a registered trademark of DeepSeek. Unauthorized use in project names can cause user misunderstanding and confusion and may constitute trademark infringement.
- Avoid using official brand materials in promotions or presentations in a way that could cause misunderstanding or suggest official endorsement, cooperation, or authorization.

A clear and orderly community environment makes each developer's work easier to recognize. We may contact parties whose use does not follow these guidelines and ask for appropriate adjustments to maintain the ecosystem's overall clarity.
